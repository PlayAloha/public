2. What Aloha is

Aloha is a social and party game platform for playing in person, with friends in the same place. You can play:

  • on one phone, passing the device around;
  • with everyone on their own phone, in a room connected over the internet and identified by a code;
  • in the Daily challenge, on your own;
  • in Aloha Party, a sequence of minigames with a scoreboard and a podium.

Aloha is not a drinking game and does not require or encourage drinking alcohol.

4. Play safely

Some games use the phone as a physical object: in On the Forehead it is held on your forehead and tilted up or down, and some Aloha Party minigames ask you to turn or tilt the device.

  • Play in a safe place, free of obstacles, and hold the device firmly.
  • Never play while driving, crossing the street or in any situation that needs your attention.
  • Respect anyone who does not want to take part in a round.

5. Rooms, nicknames and typed answers

In connected rooms, each person picks a nickname and a tiki (avatar), which the other players in the room can see. In Fake Answer, typed answers appear on the screens of the other players in the same room.

You are responsible for what you write. Do not use nicknames or answers that:

  • offend, harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone;
  • contain sexual, violent or illegal content;
  • expose personal data about you or others (phone number, address, documents);
  • impersonate someone else.

The server filters offensive words, and the room host can hide an answer. No filter is perfect: if something offensive shows up, tell us at dezoapps@gmail.com, with the room code and approximate time if possible. We review reports and may block access for anyone who breaks these Terms.

Room content is not kept after the room ends (see the Privacy Policy).
