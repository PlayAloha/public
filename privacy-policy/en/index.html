Aloha Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Português · English · Español

This Policy explains which data the Aloha app processes, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how you can exercise your rights. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and also takes into account the rules of other countries where the app is available.

Versions: Português · Español. If the versions differ, the Portuguese version prevails.

1. Summary

  • Aloha has no account and no login. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number or ID.
  • For rooms and purchases to work, the server creates an anonymous identifier for your installation.
  • What you type in a room (nickname and answers) is kept only while the room exists.
  • We measure usage only if you enable Share usage data during the current app launch; minimal events go to Aloha's Cloudflare server for aggregate analysis. We use Firebase Crashlytics (crashes), Google AdMob (ads in the free version) and RevenueCat (purchases).
  • The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.
  • We do not sell personal data.

2. Controller and contact

Controller: Vinícius Brilhante Dezotti, the developer of Aloha, in Brazil. Privacy contact, including as data protection officer (LGPD art. 41): dezoapps@gmail.com.

3.2 Connected rooms

When you create or join a room, the Aloha server (Cloudflare) receives and keeps, only for the life of the room:

  • the room code;
  • your nickname (up to 20 characters), your tiki and your seat color;
  • random player and session identifiers;
  • the match state, including typed answers in Fake Answer and votes.

Your nickname, tiki and answers are seen by the other players in the same room. The room and everything in it is deleted when everyone leaves, when no one is left after a one-minute reconnection window, or after 6 hours without activity. The anonymous access identifier (3.3) is never shown to other players.

3.3 Anonymous identifier and purchases

The first time the app needs it, the server creates a random anonymous identifier for the installation and a secret key (the server keeps only a hashed version of the key). Linked to this identifier, the server keeps:

  • whether the demo Party has been used, and the start and end of Parties created;
  • a summary of your purchases: plan, expiry, purchase date, product and transaction identifiers provided by the store and by RevenueCat.

We use this data to unlock and restore paid access, prevent fraud and provide support. It is kept for as long as needed for these purposes and to meet legal and tax obligations. You can ask us to delete it (section 7).

3.4 IP address and connection data

Every internet connection reveals an IP address. The Aloha server uses the IP in the moment, to limit abuse (for example, how many rooms one address can create per minute) and to protect the service; Aloha does not store the IP in rooms, with the anonymous identifier or in the usage metrics dataset. Cloudflare, which hosts the server, may keep technical edge logs for a limited time under its own policy. Ad and purchase providers also process the IP.

3.5 Usage metrics (Aloha server)

To understand which games are played and improve the app, we send events to Aloha's server only if you enable Settings → Share usage data. The permitted events are:

  • screens opened, by the screen's generic name (never the room code);
  • match start and end and Party creation: mode, game, number of players and duration;
  • purchase screen opened and where from, purchases (which plan), restores;
  • a store review request and the number of completed matches before that request;
  • rewards received (which feature).

The server accepts only permitted event types and parameters. The Cloudflare usage metrics dataset stores event names, categories and counts; it does not store an installation identifier, IP address, nicknames, names, room codes or typed text. The IP is used temporarily to limit abuse at the server entrance and may appear in Cloudflare's technical edge logs (section 3.4).

Without an affirmative choice during the current app launch, no usage metric is sent, in any region. The setting starts off each time you open the app, and you need to enable it again if you want to share usage data. We do not save a positive authorization to restore it automatically; this prevents an old state from re-enabling collection after a failure to save a withdrawal. A choice made in an earlier version that used Firebase Analytics is not carried over either. We do not hold earlier events to send later. You can withdraw your choice in Settings → Share usage data; new sends stop immediately. Google's ad choices are separate and do not enable usage metrics.

3.6 Crashes (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes, Crashlytics receives a technical report: the error, where in the code it happened, app version, device model and OS, and an installation identifier. We use it only to fix bugs.

3.7 Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version may show Google AdMob ads. To select, show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may process:

  • the device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow it; advertising ID on Android);
  • IP, approximate country, device model and OS, language;
  • interactions with ads.

How you stay in control:

  • iOS: before the system prompt, Aloha explains what it is for; you can decline without losing anything in the game. Without permission, ads do not use the IDFA.
  • Android: you can delete or reset the advertising ID and turn off ad personalization in your device's Google settings.
  • Europe, UK and Switzerland: a Google consent form appears before any ad. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad choices.

Aloha's ads are limited to content suitable for teens (Google's “T” rating). Subscribers and guests in a Premium Party do not see ads.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

3.8 Sharing by you

When you invite someone to a room or share a Daily challenge result, the app opens the system share menu with a text (the room code or the result grid). Only what you choose is sent, to whom you choose.

3.9 Sensors

Some games use the accelerometer and gyroscope to detect whether the phone was tilted or turned. This data is used only on the device, during the play, and is not sent or stored.

3.10 Support

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you send. The support button on the purchase screen includes the anonymous identifier so we can find your purchase.

These providers may process data outside Brazil, mainly in the United States. Transfers rely on the providers' contractual safeguards (such as standard clauses) and on what is needed to provide the service you asked for.

7. Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • confirm whether we process your data and access it;
  • correct data;
  • ask for anonymization, blocking or deletion;
  • ask for portability;
  • know who we share data with;
  • withdraw consent (ATT in iOS settings; the form in Settings → Ad choices; metrics in Settings → Share usage data);
  • complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD) or to the authority in your country.

Since there is no account, for requests about the anonymous identifier or purchases, write to dezoapps@gmail.com from the support button on the purchase screen (it already includes the identifier), or include the store's transaction ID. We reply within 15 days. Data controlled by Apple, Google or RevenueCat can also be requested from them directly.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the ads described in 3.7, which you can limit as described there.

8. Children and teens

Aloha is intended for people 13 years of age or older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child used the app and sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

For teens, we process the minimum necessary, in their best interest, and ads are limited to suitable content.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS and WSS), keep the device credential in secure storage, store only a hash of the secret key on the server, and the room never sends a phone what it should not see (such as who the impostor is). No system is completely secure; if a relevant incident happens, we will notify you as required by law.
